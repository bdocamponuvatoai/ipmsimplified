# Asset provenance and review

Generated with the built-in image generator for this project. No image search or stock provider was used.

Audience-photo direction: documentary commercial photography for infrastructure compliance software; 1600×1200, 4:3; natural slightly cool daylight; slate-blue shadows; sky-blue highlights; realistic muted materials; 35mm look; subtle grain; calm, competent, unposed. No readable text, logos, insignia, seals, branded uniforms, identifiable real people, flames or emergencies.

- A1 / `a1-jurisdictions`: A plan reviewer at a desk with printed abstract fire alarm drawings and an unreadable monitor, municipal office, face turned away.
- A2 / `a2-contractors`: A technician in plain dark workwear testing a sprinkler riser valve in a clean mechanical room, holding a rugged tablet. Real steel, red pipe and brass valve materials.
- A3 / `a3-owners`: A property manager walking through a glass-and-concrete lobby, reviewing a tablet, face partly turned away.
- Hero / `ipm-systems-hero`: A 2048×768 wide architectural systems portrait with a deep-navy copy field, a modern glass-and-concrete building, three abstract records and linked inspection, permit and maintenance infrastructure. The editable PNG master is retained in `assets/source/`; only optimized AVIF and WebP derivatives are publicly served.

The generated outputs were visually inspected in the conversation for composition and conspicuous anatomical errors. They are illustrations, not evidence of actual customers or product use. The technical geometry in A2 must be reviewed by a human subject-matter expert. The brief's human sign-off has not occurred.

All final AVIF and WebP exports are included in `public/photos/`; sizes are recorded in `content/photo-sizes.json`. The optional preparation script refers to session source PNGs, which are not required to build the repository. Final assets are already included.

The inline building, all eight program icons, monogram, wordmark, record lines and interface mockups are code authored for this project. Static brand exports are in `public/icons/`.

The favicon set is generated from the responsive three-node IPM logo in `app/icon.svg`. Its simplified P/I/M glyphs preserve the brand structure and remain legible at browser-tab size. The generator writes 16, 32, 180, 192 and 512px PNGs plus `app/favicon.ico`. Run `node scripts/generate-favicons.mjs` after changing the source.
