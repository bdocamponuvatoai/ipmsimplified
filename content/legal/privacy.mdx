<h2 id="information">Information we collect</h2>

When you request a walkthrough or contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, work email, organisation, role, product interests and message. Our hosting provider may also process standard request information, including IP address, browser details and timestamps, for security and service operation.

The site does not use advertising trackers and does not sell personal information.

<h2 id="use">How we use information</h2>

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, arrange requested conversations, operate and secure the website, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use an enquiry to subscribe you to unrelated marketing without permission.

<h2 id="sharing">Service providers</h2>

We use service providers only where needed to operate the site. Depending on deployment configuration, these may include the hosting platform, Resend for email delivery and Upstash for rate limiting. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

<h2 id="retention">Retention and choices</h2>

We retain enquiry records only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain appropriate business records and meet legal obligations. Abuse-prevention identifiers are hashed before rate-limit processing. You may ask to access, correct or delete information associated with your enquiry, subject to applicable law and necessary recordkeeping.

The website is intended for business users and is not directed to children. Information may be processed in locations where our service providers operate.

<h2 id="contact">Contact</h2>

For privacy questions or requests, email [contact@ipmsimplified.com](mailto:contact@ipmsimplified.com).
